# Asiri

**Compliance infrastructure for Nigeria's Data Protection Act (NDPA).**

[Asiri](https://asiri.ng) is a trust and compliance operations platform for NDPA-regulated businesses. We turn Nigeria's data protection requirements into software: automated compliance workflows, audit-ready evidence, and public Trust Centers — with white-label tooling for licensed Data Protection Compliance Organisations (DPCOs).

## What We Build

- **Compliance operations** — structured NDPA workflows instead of spreadsheets: registration, ROPA, DPIAs, and audit preparation
- **Audit evidence** — continuously collected, regulator-ready documentation
- **Trust Centers** — public pages where companies demonstrate their compliance posture to customers and partners
- **DPCO tooling** — white-label infrastructure for Nigeria's licensed compliance organisations

Data residency matters in this market: Asiri runs on AWS af-south-1 (Cape Town), keeping data on the African continent.
